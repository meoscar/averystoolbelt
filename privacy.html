Privacy

Avery's Toolbelt is an animated series for children aged 3-6, made by one independent creator.

This website collects nothing. No sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. The coloring pages are plain PDF files you download.

Our YouTube channel marks every video as made for kids. Viewing on YouTube is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The YouTube API. We use the YouTube Data API only to upload and manage videos on our own channel. We do not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other channel. See also YouTube's Terms of Service.

Social pages and our shop (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Redbubble) are run by those companies under their own policies. We never ask children to message us or to share their name, photo or location; if a child writes in, we answer the grown-up.

Questions: message us on Facebook.
